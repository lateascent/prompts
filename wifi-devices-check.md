# What is connected to your home Wi-Fi (advice only)

Open the list of devices on your router, type the names into an AI chat, get them sorted into known and unknown. The AI only advises. You change the settings.

## 2 minutes, no tools

- Open your router app, or type the router address in your phone browser while on your home Wi-Fi (often 192.168.0.1 or 192.168.1.1, check the router sticker or your provider's page).
- Find "Connected devices", "Client list" or "Device list".
- Type the device names. Do not upload a screenshot: it can show your Wi-Fi name, addresses and names of people.
- Never send a photo of the router sticker to any AI chat: it often shows the default password.

## Prompt

```
Devices on my home Wi-Fi: [paste names].
Sort them into phones and computers, TV and smart home, and unknown. For each unknown one, tell me how to find out what it is.
Advice only. I change the settings myself.
```

## Next step

If your router still uses the admin password from the sticker, change it in the router settings (often under Administration or System). Update the router firmware if it offers one. Consider a guest network for smart TVs and other smart devices.

## Rules

- Do not paste passwords, codes or card numbers into any AI chat.
- An unknown device is not always a threat: many phones and smart plugs show a technical name. Check before you block anything.
