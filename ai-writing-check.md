# Does your text sound like AI? (3-line check)

AI detectors judge the text, not how you wrote it, and they can flag text a person wrote. Nothing here guarantees a detector will not flag you. What it does: it shows you the lines that read like AI, so you can fix them yourself, and it tells you how to keep proof that you wrote the text.

## 1. The 3-line check

Paste this into any AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), then paste your text under it:

```
Find every line in my text that sounds like AI writing.
Quote it, name the pattern, give a plainer version in a few words.
Do not rewrite anything else and do not add facts.
```

The AI only points at lines. You make the edits, in your own words.

## 2. The patterns I cut from every post

**Punctuation and format**
- Em dashes (—). Use a period or a comma.
- Emojis, hashtags, bold, headers in places that do not need them.

**Words**
delve, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, robust, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, tapestry, realm, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, intricate, paramount, transformative, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness, ever-evolving, unlock, unleash.

**Phrases**
it's worth noting, at the end of the day, when it comes to, at its core, in today's world, the reality is, the truth is, here's the thing, let me be clear, let's dive in, what most people miss.

**Patterns**
- "It's not X, it's Y." Say Y directly.
- A fake reveal with a colon: "The best part: it learns." Write a normal sentence.
- "...highlighting / underscoring / showcasing..." Cut it or say the real result.
- Puffery: "a pivotal moment", "a testament to", "plays a vital role". State the fact.
- "Experts agree", "studies show" with no source. Name the source or cut it.
- Setups: "What if I told you...", "Think about it:".
- Slogan endings: "Let that sink in." End on a fact.
- Recap endings: "In conclusion", "Ultimately".
- Every sentence the same length. Mix short and long.
- General words where a detail fits. Use names, numbers, your own example.

## 3. If you get flagged: keep proof

1. **Google Docs:** File, Version history, See version history. Your edits are listed by time.
2. **Word:** version history works for files saved in OneDrive or SharePoint: File, Info, Version History.
3. Keep your notes, outlines and drafts, and offer to walk through the paper in person.

Start writing in Google Docs or in a OneDrive file from the first line, so the history exists before anyone asks.

## Rules

- The AI only reads and points. Do not let it rewrite your paper and hand that in.
- Check your school's or client's AI policy before you use any AI tool on the text.
